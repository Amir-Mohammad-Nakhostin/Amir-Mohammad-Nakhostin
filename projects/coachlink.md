# CoachLink

**Sports technology · SaaS · Co-founder & technical lead**

CoachLink is a sports management platform focused on workflows for coaches, athletes and sports facilities.

- Product focus: coaching management and digital services for the sports ecosystem.
- Engineering focus: full-stack application development, backend services, data management and iterative user-experience improvements.
- Role: co-founder and technical lead (CTO).
- Project website: [coachlinkapp.ir](https://coachlinkapp.ir).
