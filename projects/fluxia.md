# FLUXIA

**Intelligent urban traffic management · Early-stage project**

FLUXIA is a planned research and engineering platform for traffic prediction, interpretable traffic analysis, simulation-based adaptive control and emergency-vehicle prioritization.

- Proposed architecture: C# operational core and Python intelligence core.
- Planned development: a local-first simulation-based competition prototype.
- Long-term direction: extensible intelligent transportation systems.
- Status: architecture and planning; implementation and performance validation remain pending.

FLUXIA is not deployed to real traffic infrastructure. No safety, accuracy or performance claim is made at this stage.
