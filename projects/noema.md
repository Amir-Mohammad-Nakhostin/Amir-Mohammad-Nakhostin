# NOEMA

**Independent language-model research · Experimental project**

NOEMA (M0) explores small language models, learning methods, memory mechanisms and reasoning-oriented architectures.

The research emphasizes controlled comparisons, reproducibility and evaluation against appropriate baselines rather than claims of frontier-model performance.

- Research questions: small-model learning, memory and reasoning.
- Engineering focus: experimental infrastructure and repeatable evaluation.
- Status: ongoing exploratory research. No claim of a demonstrated performance advantage or peer-reviewed publication is made here.

The working repository is private. Validated results and shareable artifacts may be published separately after review.
